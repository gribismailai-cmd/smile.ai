# DentPath disclaimer (DRAFT – not reviewed by a lawyer)

DentPath is an independent planning tool. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or approved by any university, dental faculty, admissions service or testing agency.

Admission requirements change every cycle. DentPath shows each value with its source and the date it was checked. Before you rely on any value, confirm it on the school's official website. The school's website is always the authority.

DentPath compares your numbers with published minimums only. It does not predict whether you will be admitted.
