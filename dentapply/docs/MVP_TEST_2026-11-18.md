## 5. Accessibility scan

| Check | Before | After fix |
|---|---|---|
| Controls without an accessible name | 0 | 0 |
| Tap targets under 44 × 44 pt (Apple guideline) | 30 (links, chips, Close, Copy, resource links) | **0** |
| Checkboxes/toggles under 44 pt | Flagged by the scan, but each sits inside a full-width label row of ≥ 52 pt, so the tap target is the row | No change needed |

Fix applied: minimum 44 × 44 pt on link buttons, filter chips, Close and resource links.

## 6. Not tested

- Real students (none recruited yet)
- Live Vercel deployment, HTTPS and security headers (not deployed)
- Safari on a real iPhone, "Add to Home Screen" and offline mode (needs a device)
- VoiceOver / TalkBack screen readers
- Data accuracy: the app shows unverified data until the tracker is filled

## 7. Verdict

The MVP **works end to end** on iPhone and Android sizes: 15/15 tasks, 39/39 automated tests, no console errors, no third-party requests. It is **not ready for students yet** because none of its data has been checked by a person and it is not deployed.
