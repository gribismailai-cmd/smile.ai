# DentPath privacy policy (DRAFT – not reviewed by a lawyer)

Last updated: [[date of publication]]

## What we collect
Nothing. DentPath has no accounts and no server that stores your information.

## What stays on your device
Your profile (GPA, test scores, toggles), checklist ticks and GPA calculator rows are saved in your browser's local storage (web app) or the app's local storage (iOS). They never leave your device. Deleting the app or clearing site data removes them.

## Third parties
- Hosting: the web app is served by [[host name]], which may log technical data such as your IP address when you load the page. See [[host privacy policy link]].
- Links to school websites open those sites, which have their own privacy policies.
- [[Remove this line once fonts are self-hosted:]] Fonts are loaded from Google Fonts, which receives your IP address.

## Analytics and ads
None.

## Children
DentPath is meant for university students. It does not knowingly collect data from anyone.
